# Exercises

Research one of the following types of wave or instability and prepare a 5 minute presentation for the class:

1. Magnetorotational instability (MRI)
2. Neutron star r-modes and the CFS instability
3. Salt-fingering or doubly-diffusive instability
4. Jeans instability
5. Thermal instability in the interstellar medium
6. Parker instability in galactic disks
7. Shear waves in solids

In your presentation you should answer the following questions:
- What is the background state being perturbed?
- What is the form of the fluid equations used? For example, which terms in particular need to be included, or are there certain approximations typically made?
- What is the dispersion relation? If instability, what is the instability criterion?
- What is the basic physics of the wave or instability?
- What are important astrophysical applications?
