## 1. Vorticity in a compressible fluid

Draw diagrams to illustrate the ideas of vortex tilting and stretching.

The derivation of equation {eq}`eq:stretchtilt` in the notes assumes an incompressible flow. Show that if compressibility is included, then the same equation holds but with $\vec{\omega}\rightarrow \vec{\omega}/\rho$. Give a physical interpretation of this result.

## 2. Rotational bulge

Write down the effective potential at the Earth's surface as the sum of the gravitational potential $-GM/r$ and centrifugal potentials, and use it to derive a formula for the height of the rotational bulge as a function of latitude $h(\theta)$.

In reality, the distortion of the Earth's shape changes the gravitational potential so that it is not spherically-symmetric. What will the correction to the gravitational potential look like? How will it change your estimate for $h(\theta)$?

## 3. Rossby number in the Sun

Estimate the Rossby number for convection inside the Sun.

Look at [Figure 5](https://www.astroexplorer.org/details/10_1086_306146_fg6) from Schou et al. (1998) which shows the rotational profile inside the convection zone as obtained from helioseismology. Is it what you would expect or not? Explain.
